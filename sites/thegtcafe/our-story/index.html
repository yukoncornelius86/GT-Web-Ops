Our Story

The journey is better together.

The GT Cafe is the coffee side of The Grand Tour Collective, a family-run Southern Maryland business founded by Mike Zanni, a U.S. Navy veteran.

Mike’s love of cars is also a love of what comes with them: shared journeys, thoughtful destinations, and time with people who enjoy the ride. The Café carries that same spirit into a quieter part of the day.

Before the miles, there’s coffee.

A cup of coffee gives us a reason to slow down. To make time for a conversation, linger a little longer, or enjoy a moment together before heading out.

Our coffees and Maryland coastal artwork bring those interests together. Familiar flavors, cars we love, and places that feel close to home—all part of a business built around care, family, and connection.

Whether your morning begins in the garage, around the kitchen table, or with a friend, we’re glad you’re here.

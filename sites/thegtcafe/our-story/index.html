Our Story

A café for the journey.

The Grand Tour Cafe is the coffee expression of The Grand Tour Collective, a family-run venture rooted in vehicles, craft and time together.

Before the miles, there’s coffee.

The first collection celebrates the places and vehicles that make a drive memorable. Southern Maryland inspires our illustrated labels. The intended roaster and dropship supplier is Temecula Coffee Roasters in California, and we do not roast these beans ourselves.

FAQ

A few things to know.

Can I place an order?

Coming soon — not yet accepting orders. Cart, checkout, and payments are disabled. We are not accepting email orders.

What’s in each bag?

Each coffee is flavored whole bean coffee, 12 oz (340 g). Ingredients: Coffee, Natural Flavorings.

Where is the coffee roasted?

Our roasting partner is Temecula Coffee Roasters in California. The GT Cafe is a Southern Maryland business; we do not roast in-house.

What is The Coastal Grand Tour?

A $69.95 collection with one 12 oz whole-bean bag each of Warm-Up Lap (Cinnabun), Ivory Overdrive (French Vanilla), and Golden Hour Grand Touring (Caramel). Save $4.90 compared with three $24.95 bags. Special gift packaging is not included or promised.

What about shipping and tax?

Standard shipping is included within the contiguous 48 states plus DC. Applicable tax is additional. Delivery and processing estimates will be shared before ordering opens.

Are the labels approved?

All three TCR labels remain pending approval. Bag imagery is illustrative; final packaging may vary.

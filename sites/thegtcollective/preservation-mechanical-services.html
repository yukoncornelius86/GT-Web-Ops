A stewardship mindset

More personal than a high-volume repair lane.

Our role is to understand the vehicle, how it is used and what matters to its owner. We combine preservation-minded detailing, practical maintenance planning and carefully scoped mechanical work.

Every recommendation is discussed before work begins. If a need falls outside our current equipment, experience or authorized scope, we will say so and help coordinate an appropriate specialist.

Care for the vehicle in front of us—not a generic service interval.
Services

Preserve the condition. Plan what comes next.

Services are evaluated individually, documented clearly and performed only when they fit GT's current capabilities.

01

Preventive maintenance

Oil and filter service, batteries, fluids and sensible maintenance planning based on condition and use.

02

Brake & fluid services

Brake pads, rotors, brake-fluid service, visible-condition observations and clear documentation.

03

Minor mechanical repair

Individually evaluated repairs that fit our current facility, tools, experience and schedule.

04

Condition assessments

Courtesy inspections and visual condition assessments—not a state inspection or safety certification.

05

Detailing & cosmetic preservation

Preservation-focused detailing, paint correction, careful cleaning and protective treatments.

06

Dry ice cleaning

Targeted removal of grime and buildup from suitable surfaces without introducing water or chemical residue.

07

Maintenance planning

Prioritized recommendations that account for the vehicle's condition, intended use and future needs.

08

Service documentation

Photography, completed-work records and a useful history that supports better decisions over time.

Clear boundaries

The right work, in the right hands.

Being useful includes being honest about what belongs with a specialty provider.

Work we evaluate personally

Preventive maintenance, brake and fluid services, minor mechanical repair, visual condition assessments, detailing, dry ice cleaning, planning and documentation.

Work we may coordinate or refer

Specialized diagnostics, major engine or transmission work, alignments, tires, state inspections, manufacturer-specific programming and other work outside our current capabilities.

Referral or coordination does not imply that outside work is performed, warranted or certified by GT.

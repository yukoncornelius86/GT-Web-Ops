Why Stewardship

Meaningful vehicles are too often cared for in fragments.

The car is serviced when something becomes urgent. Notes, photographs and recommendations live in different places. No single person holds the full picture over time.

01

Reactive service

Attention arrives only when a warning light, deadline or obvious concern makes the decision urgent.

02

Scattered history

Completed work, photographs and recommendations become difficult to find or use when the next decision arrives.

03

No continuous view

Different providers see one moment in the vehicle's story, but no one is actively learning the whole vehicle over time.

Stewardship connects the care, the decisions and the history.
Preservation Baseline & GT Stewardship

Start with a clear baseline. Build an ongoing care plan.

Document condition, priorities, risks, and the next sensible preservation steps before committing to work. That baseline becomes the starting point for GT Stewardship: thoughtful reviews, a practical care plan, and a useful vehicle history. What we review

Your priorities, available history, and accessible vehicle condition. The agreed scope sets the areas to assess and document.

What you receive

A documented starting point with photographs, observations, and practical priorities for the vehicle's next care decisions.

How it is arranged

The assessment scope and any fee are agreed before it begins. It can start a standalone project or an ongoing relationship; we confirm what is included in your selected plan.

Find your fit

The right relationship depends on how you enjoy the car.

Every package is personal. This is a simple starting point; we will confirm the right plan after learning about the vehicle and your priorities.

Steady oversight

One meaningful vehicle

A straightforward rhythm for an enthusiast car that benefits from twice-yearly attention, planning and a useful record.

Higher-touch care

A frequently enjoyed favorite

More regular check-ins and included preservation care for an owner who wants GT closer to the vehicle throughout the year.

Coordinated care

Two or three vehicles

A single organized view across a small collection, with each covered vehicle receiving attention appropriate to its use.

Concierge
by choice.
Never by default

This is true concierge service, not an auto renewal.

As the current term approaches its end, we will review the vehicle's care, completed services and future priorities with you. We are confident you will find the relationship worth continuing, but a new term begins only with your explicit approval.

Stewardship is ongoing care, planning and documentation. It is not insurance, a manufacturer warranty, a formal safety certification or a guarantee against mechanical failure.

GT Stewardship

An ongoing vehicle-care relationship for owners who want help monitoring, preserving, maintaining, and documenting their vehicles over time - with a consistent point of contact who learns the vehicle and its history.

The relationship

Care that builds useful history.

Monitor

Scheduled condition reviews, identification of developing concerns, service reminders, and preventive-maintenance planning.

Preserve

Preservation-focused detailing and included services appropriate to the selected plan.

Document

Photography and an organized record of completed work, recommendations, and the vehicle's evolving needs.

Priority scheduling, preferred pricing, service frequency, and included benefits vary by plan. Stewardship is not insurance, a manufacturer warranty, a formal safety certification, or a guarantee against mechanical failure.

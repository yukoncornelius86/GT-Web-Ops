AFTER THE WORK

Protection is
an ongoing practice.

WoolWax is a maintenance treatment, not a one-and-done promise. A little attention to its care is part of looking after the truck.

WoolWax manufacturer guidance

01

A soft, active film.

WoolWax remains soft and pliable rather than drying into a hard coating.

02

Clean gently.

For a maintenance refresh, the manufacturer recommends a garden-hose rinse—not removing or power-washing the existing coating.

03

Inspect. Maintain. Repeat.

Re-treatment needs depend on use and location. Ask the crew about an inspection and a maintenance plan for your vehicle.

Preservation first

Correction and protection should support the same outcome.

If the paint is cloudy or swirled before protection, appropriate correction and polishing may be part of the preparation. GT selects the least-aggressive process that supports the target result.

Common questions

Can paint preservation make my car look new?

It can dramatically improve gloss and clarity, but the final result depends on paint thickness, defect depth, prior damage, and how much clear coat can safely be polished.

How does polishing fit into the program?

Polishing is used when the assessment and desired outcome call for it. It sits inside a larger scope that can include decontamination, defect correction, ceramic protection, trim, glass and wheel protection, and documentation.

How is the project quoted?

GT quotes the complete preservation outcome after reviewing the vehicle, paint condition, prior repairs, target result, and protection system.

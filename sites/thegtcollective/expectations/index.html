The promise

Premium does not mean unlimited.

It means the right work, performed carefully, within a scope that both sides understand.

Automotive condition is rarely uniform. A project can uncover previous repairs, fragile finishes, corrosion, access limitations, or contamination that photographs did not show. Those discoveries deserve decisions—not silent assumptions.

  • Discreet calibration area before broader cleaning
  • Matched intake and final photography
  • Final walk-through and condition observations

Not included unless added

  • Mechanical disassembly or repair
  • Corrosion removal, treatment, or refinishing
  • Replacement of failed coatings, seals, or insulation
  • Forced removal of permanent staining or patina
  • Concours refinishing or hardware replacement
  • Work behind inaccessible shields or assemblies
What can change the plan

Condition is allowed to speak. Scope is not allowed to drift.

Any condition that meaningfully changes risk, access, effort, timing, or likely outcome triggers a scope gate.

01

Hidden contamination

Heavy oil, adhesive, undercoating, nesting material, or layered deposits can require a different method or a narrower finish line.

02

Fragile material

Lifting paint, brittle labels, soft coatings, aged rubber, exposed wiring, or previous repairs can require protection, exclusion, or specialist review.

03

Restricted access

Shields, covers, ride height, modifications, immobilization, and fasteners may limit what can be cleaned or photographed without disassembly.

04

A new objective

A request to move from preservation to repair, refinishing, or concours presentation is a new scope—not a small add-on assumed during the job.

The change protocol

You remain part of the decision.

See it

We send a clear photograph or walk you through the condition.

Understand it

We explain the consequence, choices, and recommendation in plain language.

Choose it

You approve a revision, defer the area, or keep the original finish line.

What the result means

Cleaner is not rebuilt. Visible is not corrected.

Responsible cleaning may make wear, corrosion, staining, repairs, and failed finishes more apparent. That visibility is often the value of the work.

  • Original finishes and visible age can remain.
  • Permanent staining and oxidation can remain.
  • Mechanical leaks or failures can remain until repaired.
  • Previously hidden condition can become easier to see.
  • Matched photography records the result without promising perfection.
Custody and delivery

The handoff should leave no mystery.

You see the work, the record, and the remaining condition together.

We review the result against the brief, share care notes, identify future priorities only where meaningful, and explain any area intentionally left untouched.

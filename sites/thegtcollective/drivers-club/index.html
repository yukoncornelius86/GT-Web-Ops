Experiences · Grand Tour Drivers Club

The best part of owning a great car
is where it takes you.

A curated regional enthusiast community connected to The Grand Tour Collective. Carefully planned drives, thoughtful destinations, good meals, interesting cars, and people worth knowing.

We’re gathering interest as we shape the first experiences. Groups will remain intentionally small and curated.

Join the interest list
The people make the drive

Enthusiasm matters.
The price of the car doesn’t.

A well-loved daily driver, a weekend classic, or a long-kept favorite can all belong here. We value attitude, participation, respect, and a shared interest in ownership and driving.

This is not an open Cars & Coffee mailing list, a discount club, an exotic-only group, or a status-led networking club. There is no giant event calendar to keep up with; each experience will have a clear purpose and a manageable group.

Reasons to get out and enjoy it.

These are the experiences we’re exploring, rather than a confirmed schedule.

GT Sunday Drive

A good morning route, a relaxed stop, and time to talk cars.

Grand Tour Day

A full day of worthwhile roads, a thoughtful destination, and a good meal.

Overnight Grand Tour

A small-group journey with room to linger and enjoy the destination.

Preservation Sessions

Practical conversations about caring for the vehicles we want to keep.

Collection / Shop Visits

Meet the people and learn the stories behind interesting cars and their care.

Auction / Reveal Nights

Shared stories, ownership perspectives, and the anticipation of what comes next.

Occasional GT Cafe gatherings will bring the same hospitality to a simpler get-together.

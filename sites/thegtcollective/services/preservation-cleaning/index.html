Method follows material

One preservation objective. Two complementary technologies.

GT selects the cleaning method based on the material and preservation objective rather than forcing one technology onto every job. A project may use dry ice, laser cleaning, conventional methods, or a carefully sequenced combination.

Every project begins with an assessment of material, finish, contamination, and desired outcome. Not every component or finish is suitable for either process.

Dry ice

Often suited to grease, oil, dirt, underbody and engine-bay contamination, delicate broad-area cleaning, and preservation work where residue-free cleaning is beneficial.

Laser

Often suited to rust, oxidation, corrosion, coatings, localized contamination, component restoration, intricate metal parts, and controlled surface cleaning.

Project applications

From complete vehicle areas to carefully evaluated components.

Vehicle preservation

Undercarriages, suspension components, engine bays, brackets, fasteners, cooling fans, and other accessible vehicle areas.

Component preservation

Flywheels, metal assemblies, restoration components, and intricate parts—after substrate, finish, access, and desired result are reviewed.

Preparation & documentation

Sale preparation, collector-car preservation, condition documentation, and cleaning that supports a broader mechanical or protection program.

Project qualification

Scoped for a coherent result.

Preservation Cleaning projects are typically scoped as part of a larger vehicle or component program. Most GT preservation projects begin around $1,000 and are quoted based on scope, access, contamination, material, and desired result.

Small isolated component inquiries may still be reviewed for existing clients, strategic work, or an approved exception; they are not the core public offer.

Help us assess the work

Share the vehicle, affected areas, whether parts are removed, current drivability, timing, photos, and the outcome you want. You do not need to choose dry ice or laser before GT evaluates the job.

Additional scope and exclusions

Before we begin

Frequently asked questions.

Is professional photography included in the $850 package?

Yes. Both packages include approximately 75–100 professionally edited still photographs. The $1,025 package adds the described motion-media production.

Is the standard Bring a Trailer seller fee included?

Yes. GT pays the standard $99 Bring a Trailer seller listing fee. Upgraded platform services or other platform charges require separate scope confirmation.

Do you guarantee platform acceptance or a sale result?

Platform acceptance and auction results are not guaranteed. The platform decides whether to accept and schedule a listing; bidders and the owner determine the outcome.

Does preparation include mechanical work or extensive detailing?

No. Those services are separately estimated and approved. Baseline cleaning is focused on photo readiness, while known condition and defects are documented for the owner's review.

Can every vehicle have a driving or undercarriage video?

Only when safe access, insurance, owner authorization, and vehicle condition permit. We confirm an appropriate media plan during intake.

When can my auction go live?

Timing depends on vehicle readiness, records, appointments, owner approval, and platform acceptance and scheduling. No launch date is guaranteed.

How do I choose a package?

Start a project review and tell us which package interests you. We confirm the selected package, price, and approved scope personally before work begins.

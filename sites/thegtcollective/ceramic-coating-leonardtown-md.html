Ceramic coating questions, answered straight

How much does ceramic coating cost here?

The ceramic package starts at $1,200, including appropriate paint preparation and interior detailing. Vehicle size, paint condition, and the agreed finish determine the final scope and price.

How long does it last?

Service life depends on the coating selected, vehicle use, storage, and washing. We explain the chosen system and its maintenance requirements at delivery.

Is it worth it for a daily driver?

It can be useful for an owner who wants easier routine washing and an organized care plan. We discuss the benefit in the context of your vehicle, usage, and expectations.

Can you coat over scratches and swirls?

Coating does not correct existing paint defects. We discuss which marks can be improved and which should remain to preserve sound paint, then agree the finish before protection is applied.

Is the interior really included?

Yes — vacuum, shampoo, clean and protect, on every coating package. If deeper restoration is needed, we quote it openly at consultation before any work begins.

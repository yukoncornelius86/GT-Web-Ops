Fit and outcomes

How do I know which level of work my vehicle needs?

Begin with the purpose: reveal condition, preserve original material, plan restoration, prepare for presentation, or solve a specific concern. We inspect the vehicle or review useful documentation, then recommend the least aggressive effective path. The consultation is designed to clarify the outcome before selecting methods.

Do you sell fixed packages?

We frame work around outcomes rather than a package ladder. The preservation brief names the vehicle, intended result, included zones, exclusions, documentation, decision gates, and expected timing. That structure makes comparison possible without pretending every vehicle starts in the same condition.

What is the difference between preservation and restoration?

Preservation aims to reveal, retain, stabilize, and document as much sound original material as practical. Restoration replaces, repairs, refinishes, or recreates material to return a vehicle toward a chosen earlier condition. Cleaning can inform restoration, but it does not silently become restoration.

Can you prepare a vehicle for concours judging?

Concours preparation begins with the event, class, judging standard, and the owner's tolerance for intervention. The work may share methods with preservation, but its presentation objective is different. We define that objective and any specialist involvement before the scope is approved.

02

Dry ice cleaning

Does dry ice cleaning use water or leave blasting media behind?

No wash water is introduced, and the solid CO₂ pellets sublimate into gas. The contamination removed from the surface still has to be controlled and collected. “No media residue” does not mean “no cleanup” or “no operator judgment.”

Is dry ice safe for every component?

No. Pressure, flow, distance, angle, temperature, and dwell time must suit the material. Loose coatings, brittle labels, aged rubber, exposed wiring, insulation, unknown repairs, and active corrosion can require a test area, protection, exclusion, or a different method.

Will dry ice remove rust, staining, or old undercoating?

It may remove loose contamination and some coatings, but it does not reverse corrosion or guarantee uniform removal of staining, oxidation, adhesive, or undercoating. Those conditions can reveal a separate repair or restoration question. The desired finish line is agreed before aggressive removal is considered.

Can you make the underside look new?

That is not the default preservation objective. A responsibly cleaned older vehicle can retain factory coatings, discoloration, wear, repairs, and patina. If a new-looking presentation is genuinely the goal, it must be discussed as restoration or concours-oriented work with the corresponding tradeoffs.

03

Scope and timing

What happens if you find something unexpected?

The affected area stops. We photograph or show you the condition, explain why it matters, outline the practical choices, and recommend a path. You decide whether to revise the scope, defer the area, involve a specialist, or keep the original finish line.

How long will the vehicle be in the studio?

Timing depends on access, contamination, material fragility, documentation, and approval gates. The preservation brief sets an expected window after the condition review. If a meaningful discovery changes that window, you hear from us before the schedule changes.

What is not included automatically?

Mechanical disassembly or repair, corrosion treatment, refinishing, replacement of failed coatings or components, work behind inaccessible assemblies, and concours-level refinement are not implied by cleaning. Any of those can be discussed and added only through a clear revision.

How is the project priced without packages?

The consultation establishes the vehicle, outcome, zones, access, known condition, documentation, and expected effort. The resulting proposal reflects that defined scope. If the scope changes, the implication is explained before the work continues. There is no price-grid shortcut because condition is the central variable.

04

Documentation and delivery

What documentation will I receive?

The agreed record can include intake views, matched final views, the areas completed, noteworthy observations, intentionally excluded zones, care notes, and future priorities. The exact deliverable is named in the preservation brief rather than assumed.

Do before-and-after photographs guarantee every difference will be dramatic?

No. Responsible preservation can produce subtle but meaningful results. The value may be clearer fasteners, readable finishes, exposed condition, or a more useful inspection record—not a theatrical color change. Matched angles keep the comparison honest.

Will you tell me what the vehicle needs next?

We will identify observations and meaningful priorities within our competence, and we will say when another specialist is better placed to evaluate an issue. The purpose is to leave you more informed, not to manufacture an automatic second project.

What should I bring to the consultation?

Bring the vehicle details, relevant history, the areas that concern you, the intended use or event, any previous work you know about, and photographs if they help. Most important, be ready to describe what a successful result should accomplish and what must not be lost.
